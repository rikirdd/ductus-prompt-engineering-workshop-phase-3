# Vision 002: It's a Match!

**From:** Chad "Chaz" Brightwell, Founder & Chief Vibes Officer, MemeCorp
**To:** The Meme-Tinder team
**Priority:** P0 (the *real* P0 this time)

---

🤯 Team. Stop what you're doing. Well, don't stop. But *listen*.

I just got off a podcast with a guy who sold a dating app for a lot of money. He said one sentence that changed my life:

> **"Nobody wants to swipe alone."**

And I realised: we've been thinking too small. Meme-Tinder isn't about memes. It's about **people**. Memes are just how they find each other. If you and I love the same memes, we have the same sense of humour, and **that is the best reason to meet anyone, ever.**

Great work on everything so far, by the way. Please keep all of it. I'm sure this is "mostly the same app". Right? 😅

Same deal as last time: five big bets, all P0.

---

## Big Bet 1: Who Am I? 🪪

My mum uses Meme-Tinder on her phone *and* on her laptop, and it thinks she's two different people. That has to stop. A person is a person, everywhere.

- Everyone gets a **profile** with a nickname and a little picture (an emoji is fine, I'm not picky).
- I want to log in on a new device and get *my* memes, *my* likes, *my* everything.
- Please don't make it complicated. I hate passwords. Nobody has ever said "I love my password".
- Everyone who has already been swiping must **keep what they've got**. I've got hundreds of likes. Don't you dare lose them. 😤

## Big Bet 2: Find My Meme People ❤️

Show me who laughs at the same things I do.

- Show me other people and a **compatibility score**: "You and Sara are 91% compatible!"
- A list of "**People like you**", best match first.
- And let me tap on someone to see the memes we both loved. That's the fun part.

## Big Bet 3: It's a Match! 🎉

This is the moment. This is the money shot. This is what the investors will talk about at dinner.

When two people are compatible enough, **both** of them get a big, celebratory "**It's a Match!**" moment. Confetti. Sound. Fireworks. Go wild. I want my mum to scream.

How compatible is "enough"? You tell me. I'll know it when I see it.

## Big Bet 4: Live! 🔴

I want the app to feel *alive*. When someone loves a meme, I want to see it **happen**. A little ticker at the bottom: "Sara just loved *It Was DNS*". "Johan just got a match!". Without refreshing the page. Like a stock exchange, but for humour.

If five people are using the app at once, it should *feel* like five people.

## Big Bet 5: Bring a Friend 🤝

Growth! The best way to find out whether you match with someone is to **invite them**.

- I want a link I can send to a friend. They click it, swipe some memes, and see straight away how compatible we are.
- It must work even if my friend has never heard of Meme-Tinder. No sign-up wall, no tutorial, just swipe.

---

## What "done" looks like

I invite my mum with a link. She opens it on her phone, swipes ten memes, and we both see "You are 84% compatible". Then, at the bottom of my screen, I see a ticker saying that she just loved my favourite meme. I scream, she screams, there is confetti.

## A few things I haven't thought through

- Is it okay if everyone can see what everyone else liked? That's the whole point, but... maybe? I don't know. People might not like it.
- What if I'm the only user? I can't match with nobody. Please make sure there's *always* someone for me to match with. Even on day one. Even on my own laptop.
- What if my mum's score is based on two memes? That doesn't feel like a match. It feels like a coincidence.
- Do the "Hot or Not" numbers and the "memes per minute" counter from last time still make sense now? I think so? Please make sure.

## Not now

Chat, payments, premium tiers, the app store, and anything I haven't mentioned. One rocket at a time. 🚀

- Chaz
